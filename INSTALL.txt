SuperBinds 0.3.1: Form abilities filter

1. Copy SuperBinds.lua and FormAbilities.lua into your existing SuperBinds addon folder.
   Replace SuperBinds.lua. Keep your existing profiles, Media and other files.
2. Open your existing SuperBinds.toc and append this line at the end:

FormAbilities.lua

   Ensure the line SuperBinds.lua remains earlier in the TOC.
3. Restart WoW to ensure the updated TOC and new file are loaded.
4. Open BIND > See All. The scope button cycles:
   Form abilities > Castable now > All learned.

Form abilities is a strict, curated membership list. In Bear Form it includes
learned bear abilities such as Frenzied Regeneration, Growl, Mangle and Ironfur.
It excludes form-switch buttons and caster spells that would shift you out.
Cooldowns, insufficient resources and missing targets do not remove members.
Cat, Moonkin and unshifted spellcasting have their own lists. Travel has no
assigned abilities. General cross-form utility remains accessible in All learned.

Castable now preserves the previous usability filter. All learned includes
unclassified abilities, form switches and utility. Search and Unbound only work
in all three modes. Binding behaviour and profile contents are unchanged.

Classification lives in FormAbilities.lua, separate from the class-agnostic
engine. New spells may need adding there; this is not an automatic discovery of
all spell-form restrictions. Only spells present in your learned spellbook show.
Profiles can override the catalogue with profile.formAbilities using the same
form-spell-ID keys and lists of ability IDs. Spell overrides are matched too.

Validation: local regression harness passed, including strict form membership,
secret/unknown form handling, resource independence and existing binding tests.
Not tested inside the live WoW client.
